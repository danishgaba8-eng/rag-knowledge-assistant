# Remote Work Policy

Harbor & Pine Logistics is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.

## Eligibility

Office-based employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week once they have completed their 90-day probation period. Warehouse, fleet and on-site operations roles are not eligible for regular remote work.

## Requesting a remote schedule

Submit a remote work request in PeopleHub. Your manager must respond within 5 working days. Approved schedules are reviewed every 6 months.

## Core hours

Remote employees must be available during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00 local time, and must keep their calendar status up to date.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, headset and monitor. Each eligible employee receives a one-time home office stipend of $400, claimed through Expensa within 60 days of schedule approval.

## Working abroad

Working from another country requires approval from HR and Legal and is limited to 20 working days per calendar year.

# Warehouse Safety SOP

Harbor & Pine Logistics is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.

## Personal protective equipment

High-visibility vests and safety boots are required at all times on the warehouse floor. Hard hats are required in the racking and loading bay areas.

## Forklifts

Only certified operators may drive forklifts. Forklift certification must be renewed every 3 years. Operators complete a pre-shift checklist before each shift.

## Manual handling

The maximum manual lift for one person is 23 kg. Heavier items need a team lift or mechanical aid.

## Incidents and near misses

Report every incident and every near miss in SafetyLog within 24 hours. Serious injuries must also be reported to the Site Safety Officer immediately by phone.

# Damage and Loss Claims SOP

Harbor & Pine Logistics is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.

## Filing deadline

Customers must report damaged goods within 7 calendar days of delivery. Claims for lost shipments must be filed within 30 days of the expected delivery date.

## Required evidence

Every damage claim needs photos of the damaged goods and the packaging, the delivery note and the commercial invoice showing the value of the goods.

## Approval

- Claims up to $500 are approved by the Customer Service Lead.
- Claims above $500 are reviewed by the Claims Team.

## Resolution target

The target is to resolve claims within 10 business days of receiving complete evidence. The customer receives a status update at least every 3 business days.

# Salary Bands 2026 (Confidential)

Harbor & Pine Logistics is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.

Access to this document is restricted to the HR team.

## Operations

- Operations Coordinator (Level 2): $48,000 to $58,000
- Dispatcher (Level 3): $55,000 to $67,000
- Operations Manager (Level 5): $82,000 to $101,000

## Corporate

- Finance Analyst (Level 3): $62,000 to $75,000
- HR Business Partner (Level 4): $70,000 to $86,000

## Rules

Offers above the midpoint of a band need approval from the Head of HR. Bands are reviewed every January.

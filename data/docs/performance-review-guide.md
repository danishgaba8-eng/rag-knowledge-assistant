# Manager Guide to Performance Reviews

Harbor & Pine Logistics is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.

## Review cycles

Performance reviews run twice a year, in April and October. Managers must complete written reviews in PeopleHub within 3 weeks of the cycle opening.

## Rating scale

Ratings use a 1 to 5 scale, where 3 means "fully meets expectations". All ratings are discussed in a calibration session with other managers in the same department before they are shared with employees.

## Merit increases

The average merit increase budget for 2026 is 3.5% of base salary. Increases above 6% need approval from the department head.

## Performance improvement plans

A performance improvement plan (PIP) lasts 60 days, with written check-ins every 2 weeks. Involve your HR Business Partner before starting a PIP.

# Shipment Tracking FAQ

Harbor & Pine Logistics is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.

## What does a tracking number look like?

Tracking numbers start with "HP-" followed by 10 digits, for example HP-4820019377.

## How often is tracking updated?

Tracking status in the customer portal, TrackPort, updates every 15 minutes while a shipment is in transit.

## Can customers get tracking data through an API?

Yes. Contract customers can use the TrackPort API. The rate limit is 600 requests per minute per API key. API keys are issued by the Customer Success team.

## Who do customers contact about a missing shipment?

Customers should contact Customer Service through TrackPort or by phone. If the shipment is lost, Customer Service opens a claim under the Damage and Loss Claims SOP.

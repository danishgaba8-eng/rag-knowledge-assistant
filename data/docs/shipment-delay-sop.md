# Shipment Delay Handling SOP

Harbor & Pine Logistics is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.

## Delay thresholds

- Delay of more than 4 hours: the dispatcher notifies the customer by email and SMS with a new estimated arrival time.
- Delay of more than 24 hours: escalate to the Operations Manager and record delay code D2 in the TMS (transport management system).
- Delay of more than 48 hours: contract customers receive a service credit of 5% of the freight charge for that shipment.

## Delay codes

- D1: weather or natural events
- D2: extended delay escalated to the Operations Manager
- D3: carrier or vehicle breakdown
- D4: customs hold

## Customer communication

All delay notifications must include the shipment tracking number, the reason code and the new estimated arrival time. Do not promise a delivery time that dispatch has not confirmed.

# Leave Policy

Harbor & Pine Logistics is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.

## Annual leave

All full-time employees receive 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours.

Leave requests must be submitted in PeopleHub at least 10 working days in advance for absences longer than three days. Shorter absences need at least 2 working days' notice.

## Carry-over

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused annual leave days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31, after which they expire. Unused days beyond the 5-day limit are forfeited on December 31 and are not paid out, except where local law requires it.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Notify your manager before your scheduled start time on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required for absences of more than 3 consecutive working days.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must start within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Notify HR at least 8 weeks before the planned start date.

## Bereavement leave

Employees may take up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for an immediate family member and up to 2 paid days for an extended family member.

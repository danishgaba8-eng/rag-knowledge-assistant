# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Harbor & Pine Logistics is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense claims in the Expensa app within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted after 30 days need written approval from the Finance Director.

An itemised receipt is required for every expense over $25. For expenses of $25 or less, a short description is enough.

## Approval limits

- Claims up to $1,000 are approved by your direct manager.
- Claims above $1,000 must also be approved by the Finance Director.

Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle. Payroll runs every two weeks.

## Meals and travel

- Daily meal allowance: $60 for domestic travel and $85 for international travel.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable.
- Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.
- Personal car use is reimbursed at $0.42 per kilometre. Parking and tolls are reimbursed at cost.

## Corporate cards

Corporate card holders must reconcile transactions in Expensa by the 5th working day of the following month. Personal purchases on a corporate card are not allowed.

# New Employee Onboarding Checklist

Harbor & Pine Logistics is a fictional company. This document is sample data for a demo.

## Day 1

- Collect your laptop and badge from the IT Helpdesk.
- Set up MFA and VaultKey.
- Meet your onboarding buddy, who is assigned by your manager before your start date.

## First week

- Complete the security awareness training in the Learning Hub within your first 5 working days.
- Read the Leave Policy, Expense Policy and IT Security SOP.

## Probation and check-ins

The probation period is 90 days. Your manager holds check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 days. At the 90-day check-in, your manager confirms whether probation has been passed.
